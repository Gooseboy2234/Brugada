Is my GPU still doing something useful — and how far is the R104Q cure?

A tracker for one long computational hunt: does the SCN5A R104Q variant destabilize the channel's NTD core, and does D84N rescue it? BenchTop turns that question into a glance — in science words, not sysadmin words.

It answers three questions in priority order: is the science moving, is the money safe, is the machine healthy — machine last, because when things are fine it's the least interesting.

Home glances at each rig; Campaign is where the science lives; Job is the one-run close-up; Funnel is the honest whole-journey map.

Why these choices

Straight from the three things that make it “for scientists”, not a sysadmin dashboard.

The funnel refuses to lie

The SIMULATION WALL is always drawn — a hard line between what a GPU can tell you and what only a wet lab can. Percentages are of the computational journey, never “% to cure”.

Every run has a heartbeat

MD shows the salt bridge, FEP shows ΔΔG, docking shows the best hit. You watch the science converge live, not a generic progress bar.

Honest about money

Home GPU-hours are free and shown in kWh; real dollars appear only for cloud rigs. The app never pretends your 4060 Ti costs Modal money.

ETA is first-class

Computed from measured ns/day, with the confidence window shown — “ETA 3h 41m · based on last 20 min”, not a spinner.

Science leads, machine trails

Every rig card answers is the science moving first, money second, thermals last — because when things are fine, util is the least interesting number.

Alerts stay rare

Only good news you were waiting for, or something that needs your hand — a failure with its real error, a budget nearing its cap. Normal is silent.
